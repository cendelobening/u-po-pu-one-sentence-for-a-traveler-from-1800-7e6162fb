One sentence, built only from things a person of 1800 already knows — wires, a letter and its answer, libraries, and a table — that still tells the literal truth about the internet today: a message crosses the world and comes back in a breath, and the world's books stand open where you sit. The entry is: "We have strung thin wires across the whole earth: along them a letter flies to the far side of the world and returns with its answer in a breath, and every library on earth stands open at your table."
